# Open Social DID-Web: Your Identity, Your Infrastructure, Your Rules

You might use Bluesky.
You might use [Blacksky](https://blacksky.community).
You might run your own [PDS](https://github.com/blacksky-algorithms/rsky/tree/main/rsky-pds).
You might even control your own data.

**But, using decentralized infrastructure is not necessarily the same thing as owning your identity.**

Ask yourself:

* Who controls your permanent identifier?
* Who has the authority to change the keys associated with your identity?
* What happens if your hosting provider disappears?
* What happens if you want to move to another service?
* Can you maintain your identity independently of the platform where you currently participate?

You can be a participant in a decentralized ecosystem while still depending on infrastructure and identity-management mechanisms controlled by other parties.

* Own your identity.
* Control your identity.
* Take your identity with you.

## DID-Web

This is where **DID-Web** (`did:web`) enters the picture.

On the ATProto ATmosphere, people are identified by a DID (decentralized identifier).
Think of it as being similar to a telephone number.

Here are some examples of DIDs:

| DID Type | Example                            |
|----------|------------------------------------|
| DID-PLC  | `did:plc:scewmn2pl3oz36mxme2b6czz` |
| DID-Web  | `did:web:example.com`              |
